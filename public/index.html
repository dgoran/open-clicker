Packed Full of Features

Everything you need to run professional presentations

📱

Mobile Clicker

Control your slides from any phone, tablet, or computer. Large, easy-to-tap buttons designed for presenters.

⏱️

Countdown Timer

Set timers for your presenters. Color changes as time runs out to help keep presentations on schedule.

📝

Speaker Notes

Display speaker notes on the clicker so presenters can reference them while presenting.

👥

Multiple Presenters

Support unlimited presenters in a single session. Perfect for panel discussions and team presentations.

🔒

Presenter Control

Lock clickers globally or suspend individual presenters. Full control over who can advance slides.

💬

Send Messages

Communicate with presenters via the clicker for quick and easy messaging during your event.

📸

Live Screenshot

Preview current slide on the clicker so presenters can reference what's on screen.

🔴

Laser Pointer

Virtual laser pointer controlled from the clicker displays a red dot on the presentation screen.

🎤

Voice Control

Experimental voice recognition. Say "next slide please" or "last slide please" to advance.
